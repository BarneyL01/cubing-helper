## Deviations from the spec
- **Pair and reverse share one commutator.** The old script ran the rule per ordered pair, so "first letter of the pair" gave different setups for a pair and its reverse
  (14 of 189 unordered pairs differed in length, 24 in setup). Here the shorter of the two is used for both (tie: alphabetically-first pair), the other is the same setup with the base halves swapped.
  Effect: level 1 mean setup 2.11 (doc 2.20), 40.8 moves per solve (doc 41.0).
- **8 exceptions, not 9.** I R, R I, L V, V L, R V, V R, V W, W V. L S was a 4-move setup only because L comes first; S L has 3 (S walks to K with D2, then L's setup), so L S now uses that and is not an exception.
- `best` ranking: fewest moves after cancelling, then B or D over F or K, then fewer L/B, then fewer setup moves.
- Extra fields (not asked for): `rotationBack` and `setupMoves`/`moves`/`setupSeen`/`coreSeen` also on `best` and `bufferMove`; stats carry cancelled move counts as well as the 8+2n figures.
