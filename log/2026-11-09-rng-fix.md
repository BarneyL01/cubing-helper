so random scrambles were badly skewed (some corner letters almost never came up: F 10 and Q 12 times in 2,000
memos). Replaced by `Math.imul` (exact 32-bit) in every log/*.mjs; letter counts are now even. Re-ran all checkers:
three-base, Orozco corners, theory, Repeat, 3-style twists, trainer, Orozco helper P (3,000 whole solves, same K/V
rule), shortcuts (134 pairs, 79% of solves, 1.21 per solve) — ALL OK. (2026-10-16-bld-check-planner.mjs is a
compare tool that needs an old bld-memo.js as argument.)

commutators per solve; helper P pairs 36 (7%, 7.4); B or P 70 (20%, 6.9); B or P + shortcuts 204 = 54% of the
list, 51% of solve pairs, 5.7 per solve; plain Orozco 7.6; full 3-style 3.8. Pairs can be mixed freely (B and P are
