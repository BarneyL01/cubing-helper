# 2026-09-27 — 2x2 Ortega

- User sent Ortega method notes (solve one side → OLL → PBL).
- Decoded and recorded:
  - **Sune / Antisune** — same cases, same algorithms as CFOP 2-look OLL.
    Reused directly.
  - **Pi** ("F (sassy x2) G"), **U** ("F (sassy) G"), **H** ("F (sassy x3)
    G") — computed from the already-known cipher (Sassy = R U R' U', F/G =
    F/F'). H's result is character-for-character the same algorithm as
    CFOP's H1 — good internal-consistency signal that the decode is right.
  - **PBL** (all 5 cases) — given directly in raw WCA notation, not the
    cipher. The two named ones (T-perm, Y-perm) matched our
    already-verified `docs/mnemonics.md` algorithms exactly, another good
    cross-check.
- **Could not decode**: "T CMLL" (`Sassy Sledge`) and "L CMLL" (`Fipgar
  Urvop`). "Sledge", "Fipgar", and "Urvop" are new named chunks that,
  unlike every other word in `docs/mnemonics.md`, were never given a
  letter-code — they don't spell out from the R/P/U/V/F/G/M/K/N table, so
  there's no way to derive them from the cipher alone. Recorded as
  `alg: null` in `data/ortega.js` with a note on the page, and asked the
  user for the letter-code or the raw algorithm rather than guess (a guess
  here specifically risks being wrong, since 2x2 orientation-only
  algorithms have multiple valid variants, unlike a fixed-target PLL).
