2x2 — Ortega

1. Solve one side (intuitive). 2. Orient the other side's corners (OLL). 3. Permute both layers (PBL).

OLL — orient the second side

The 7 corner-orientation shapes — several are the same algorithms as CFOP's 2-look OLL.

PBL — permute both layers

Bottom layer is drawn as if you flipped the cube over sideways — the front stays at the bottom of the picture.
