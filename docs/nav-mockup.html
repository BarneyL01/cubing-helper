Cubing Helper navigation mockup

A clickable mockup of the proposed navigation: a Menu button that opens every section and page, a quiet row for the pages inside a section, and one "On this page" control. Click anything in the mocked site, or pick a page below to jump straight to it. "Desktop · top bar" is the earlier version, kept for comparison.

Tier 1 · sections

Top menu

Phone: the Menu button opens an accordion; one-page sections are plain links, others open with + and the current one starts open. Desktop (menu panel): the same Menu button opens a vertical panel with every section and page listed at once, grouped; one-page sections are single bold links, sections with several pages show their pages indented beneath. The header shows which section you are in. The top-bar version is kept for comparison.

Tier 2 · pages in a section

Section row

Only in sections with more than one page (CFOP, Roux, Blind, Commutators, Practice). Smaller and greyer than the top menu so it never reads as the main menu. At most 6 labels.

Tier 3 · this page

On this page

Phone: one compact control that stays under the header and opens the headings list. Desktop: a quiet list in the right column. It replaces today's pill bars, step bars and "Jump to" bars. Breadcrumbs go: the filled-in section and the section row already show where you are.
