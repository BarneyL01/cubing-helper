3x3 — Marshall Method

Philip Marshall's edges-first method: all twelve edges first, then the corners, with one short edge series and one eight-move corner series. The "Ultimate Solution".

Not from your own notes. This is Philip Marshall's method, from his page The Ultimate Solution to Rubik's Cube (latest revision 2005). The text is reworded; his figures are not reproduced, so the pictures are ours. Every sequence and claim was checked in the simulator. Steps 2 to 5 rely on judgement and are not checked as a whole method; the list at the end says what is not checked. The two algorithms at the end are not Marshall's: a corner twist and an edge flip, from published sources.
