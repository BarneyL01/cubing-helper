A group: helper D's bases with U2

Three bases you nearly know already: helper D's [R' D^k R , U'] with U2 as the interchange. The D turn picks G, P or S as before; the other letter is A instead of D.

Worked out here (2026-11-16), not from your notes or a published list. Proposed as a replacement for helper P's mixed set (two R U R' bases and one R' D' R); the walk rule, Buffer moves and Pair lookup pages still use helper P for now. Every commutator was checked in the cube simulator (it cycles the buffer and the two letters and moves nothing else). Pairs: setups of up to 3 moves, the shortest with R, U, F and D, or with L or B when that is at least one move shorter. Numbers weighted by how often a pair comes up in 2,000 random solves. The three bases

Same insertion and the same D turn as helper D, only the interchange changes: U' brings D into the buffer's place, U2 brings A. [R' D' R , U2] is the one base helper P already had.
