Edge cycles

Each pair of edge letters is one 3-cycle with the buffer UF, built from a commutator in the table below.

Not from your notes. Every commutator, flip and exercise is generated and checked in the simulator by log/2026-10-16-bld-cycles-generate.mjs. The buffer is UF (C and I), not the DF buffer the Reference tab uses for M2.

1. From a scramble to cycles

  1. Find the buffer's letters. UF is the buffer. Its stickers are C (U face) and I (F face).
  2. Follow the chain. The sticker in the buffer's C spot names the first target. Then take the edge sitting on that letter's spot, and so on, until a target is C or I.
  3. Pair the letters in order. Each pair is one 3-cycle with the buffer (table in section 6).
  4. An odd number of letters is covered in section 4.

2. When the cycle closes early

If the chain returns to the buffer early, start a new chain at an unsolved edge. Its first letter (the break letter, underlined) starts it and runs until a letter of that edge comes up again. Either sticker of the edge can be the break.

Pick the break that gives the fewest total moves (table below). Without the table, take the edge's first letter in letter order, as on the Practice tab.

3. Flipped edges

A flipped edge in its own place is left out of the letters and fixed at the end with a flip row. The flip M' U M' U M' U2 M U M U M U2 flips UF and UB in place and nothing else.

  • A flip row flips two edges: the buffer UF and one other edge K. Its setup brings K to UB without moving UF; undo it after the flip.
  • The number of flipped edges is always even, so the buffer's flip comes out right once every flipped edge has its row.

4. An odd number of letters

The number of edge letters is odd exactly when the number of corner letters is odd.

Corners with an odd count: use the parity step on the Twists & parity tab. It solves every corner in the tests.

Edges with an odd count: not on this page yet. A full solve with an odd count needs a method that has not been checked, so this tab does not teach one.

5. Choosing a setup move

Choose a setup as for corners (Corner cycles, section 6). Edges can also use a slice (M, E or S) as the interchange. The commutator [M', U2] is the shortest edge cycle, with four moves.
