3. Easiest: put the corners in place, twist later

If corners may end up twisted, you need one commutator per pair of corners (12, not 108). Fix the twists at the end.

  1. Memo by corners. Find where the buffer's piece belongs, then the piece in that corner, and so on. If the chain returns to the buffer early, start again from the first corner still out of place.
  2. Pair them up. Each card sends the buffer's piece to the first corner and that corner's piece to the second. Orientation can wait.
  3. Twist. Every corner is in place, some twisted. Fix them with (R' D' R D)×2 U (D' R' D R)×2 U' (U2 or U' for the other top corners; R before and R' after to include the buffer). Or see the Twists & parity tab.

These cards are the same for both setup choices: two of them use L2.
