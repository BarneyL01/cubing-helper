1. Twisted corners

A corner that is in its own place but twisted is left out of the letters. It is fixed at the end with a twist row. Directions are seen from outside the corner: clockwise means its U or D sticker moves on to the next face clockwise.

  • Each twist row changes exactly two corners: the buffer and one other corner K. The buffer changes the other way, so the total twist stays zero.
  • To fix K twisted clockwise, use the row whose K turns anticlockwise (and so the buffer turns clockwise).
  • Two twisted corners: use one row for each. The buffer's twists cancel out.

Two twisted corners with Sassy

Without a buffer: this twists two corners of the bottom layer, one each way, and leaves every other piece solved. It is built from Sassy only: Sassy twice, a bottom turn, Sassy backwards twice, the bottom turn back. Sassy twice twists the front-right bottom corner (and upsets some top pieces); the bottom turn brings the other twisted corner to front-right; Sassy backwards twice twists that one the other way and puts the top pieces back.

  1. Hold the cube so both twisted corners are in the bottom layer, one of them at front-right (DFR).
  2. Look at where the DFR corner's bottom colour is: on the right face → start with Sassy twice; on the front face → start with Sassy backwards twice.
  3. The middle turn brings the other corner to front-right: D for DFL, D2 for DBL, D' for DBR. The last turn undoes it.

The same idea with the bottom and right faces, (R' D' R D)×2 U (D' R' D R)×2 U', twists two top-layer corners (UFR and the one the U turn brings in). The R/U version above is the same sequence with the roles of U and D swapped. Sassy ×4 does the same as Sassy backwards ×2 (Sassy ×6 changes nothing), so (R U R' U')×2 D (R U R' U')×4 D' also works: 26 turns, but only Sassy. All rows are checked by log/2026-10-22-sassy-twist-check.mjs.

2. An odd number of letters

The corner swap from the Reference tab, R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R, swaps UBL with DFR (the buffer) and also swaps two edges. With an odd number of corner letters, the last letter Z is done like this:

  1. Turn the cube with a U, L or B setup that brings Z's sticker to E (the L sticker of UBL). These moves leave the buffer DFR where it is.
  2. Do the swap algorithm.
  3. Undo the setup. Z's sticker and the buffer sticker V have swapped places, so the cube has the last corner home.

Two edges end swapped by this algorithm: UL and UB with no setup, other edges when a setup is used. The Edge cycles tab explains what is and is not covered on the edge side.
