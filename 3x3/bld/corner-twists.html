1. Twisted corners

A corner that is in its own place but twisted is left out of the letters. It is fixed at the end with a twist row. Directions are seen from outside the corner: clockwise means its U or D sticker moves on to the next face clockwise.

  • Each twist row changes exactly two corners: the buffer and one other corner K. The buffer changes the other way, so the total twist stays zero.
  • To fix K twisted clockwise, use the row whose K turns anticlockwise (and so the buffer turns clockwise).
  • Two twisted corners: use one row for each. The buffer's twists cancel out.

2. An odd number of letters

The corner swap from the Reference tab, R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R, swaps UBL with DFR (the buffer) and also swaps two edges. With an odd number of corner letters, the last letter Z is done like this:

  1. Turn the cube with a U, L or B setup that brings Z's sticker to E (the L sticker of UBL). These moves leave the buffer DFR where it is.
  2. Do the swap algorithm.
  3. Undo the setup. Z's sticker and the buffer sticker V have swapped places, so the cube has the last corner home.

Two edges end swapped by this algorithm: UL and UB with no setup, other edges when a setup is used. The Edge cycles tab explains what is and is not covered on the edge side.
