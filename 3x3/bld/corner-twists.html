Twists & parity

Twisted corners and an odd number of corner letters. Neither is a 3-cycle, so they are done outside the pairs on the Corner cycles and Corner table tabs.

Not from your notes. The twist rows and parity setups are generated and checked in the simulator by log/2026-10-16-bld-cycles-generate.mjs. Buffer DFR (V, K, P).

1. Twisted corners

A twisted corner in its own place is left out of the letters and fixed at the end with a twist row. Clockwise is seen from outside the corner: its U or D sticker moves on to the next face clockwise.

  • A twist row changes two corners: the buffer and one other corner K, in opposite directions.
  • To fix K twisted clockwise, use the row whose K turns anticlockwise (and so the buffer turns clockwise).
  • Two twisted corners: use one row for each. The buffer's twists cancel out.

Two twisted corners with Sassy

This twists two bottom-layer corners, one each way, and leaves everything else solved. It uses only Sassy: Sassy twice, a bottom turn, Sassy backwards twice, the bottom turn back.

  1. Hold the cube so both twisted corners are in the bottom layer, one of them at front-right (DFR).
  2. Check the bottom colour of the DFR corner: on the right face, start with Sassy twice; on the front face, start with Sassy backwards twice.
  3. The middle turn brings the other corner to front-right: D for DFL, D2 for DBL, D' for DBR. The last turn undoes it.

The same idea with the bottom and right faces, (R' D' R D)×2 U (D' R' D R)×2 U', twists two top-layer corners. Sassy ×4 does the same as Sassy backwards ×2 (Sassy ×6 changes nothing), so (R U R' U')×2 D (R U R' U')×4 D' also works: 26 turns, Sassy only. Checked by log/2026-10-22-sassy-twist-check.mjs.

2. An odd number of letters

The Reference tab's corner swap, R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R, swaps UBL with DFR and also two edges. With an odd number of letters, do the last letter Z like this:

  1. Set up with U, L or B turns so Z's sticker is at E (the L sticker of UBL). They leave DFR alone.
  2. Do the swap algorithm.
  3. Undo the setup. Z's sticker and V have swapped, so the last corner is home.

The algorithm also swaps two edges: UL and UB with no setup, others with a setup. The Edge cycles tab says what is not covered.
