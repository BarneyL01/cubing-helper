Cubing Helper

Orozco with helper F

Your Orozco with F in B's place, done from one rotation: z, then [R' D' R , U / U' / U2], then z'. The setups are D and B turns before the rotation, never more than two.

Worked out here (2026-11-17), not from your notes or a published list. Replaces the A group page (its setups were often three moves). F is the other sticker of helper D's corner, so the setups are the same kind as helper D's: D and B turns, which never move the buffer or F. Every commutator was checked in the cube simulator, the z view was checked to be the same moves, and Orozco with helper F solved 3,000 whole random solves (M2 edges, then these corners). Built by log/2026-11-17-orozco-f.mjs.

The idea

  • Memo from C as always. The 1st letter of each pair forwards (buffer → letter → F), the 2nd backwards (buffer → F → letter).
  • Do the setup first (D and B turns, as the cube is), then z, the base, z', and undo the setup.
  • After z the base is [R' D' R , U^k]: the insertion of helper P's A base, with the U turn picking E, G or H. Without the rotation it is [U' R' U , L^k].
