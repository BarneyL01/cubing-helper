Which group?

Which of the four Orozco groups (B, D, F, K) to use for any pair of corner letters, in three levels. Look up one pair on Pair lookup.

Worked out here (2026-11-16 to 2026-11-18), not from your notes or a published list. All 378 commutators were checked in the cube simulator (each cycles the buffer and the two letters and moves nothing else), and 3,000 whole solves using this table all came out solved. Built by log/2026-11-18-pair-rule.mjs; spec in docs/four-helper-picking-rule.md. A pair and its reverse share one setup. Move counts do not include cancellations or the rotation. The rule in three levels

All 12 bases

Three bases per helper. "Position first" is the pair position → helper (forwards); "Helper first" is helper → position (backwards): the same base with its halves swapped. Helpers F and K are written as you see them after their rotation.

Level 0: the pair has B, D, F or K

Level 1: one turn into a helper's place

Level 2: one more turn first
