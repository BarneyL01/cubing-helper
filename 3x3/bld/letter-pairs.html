Letter pairs

One word for every pair of memo letters: picture the word instead of saying two letters. X is said “sh”. Each word starts with the first letter and has the second letter after it.

Our own list, made for this site (2026-10-23): 552 pairs (24 letters × 23). Words were picked to be easy to picture, and no two share a stem or mean nearly the same thing, so two pairs don't blur together. Where no good single word fits, there is a two-word phrase. Swap any word for one that works better for you. Checked by log/2026-10-23-letter-pairs-check.mjs.

Practise them in Blind drills → Memo, with “Show the letter-pair words” ticked.
