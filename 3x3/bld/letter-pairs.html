Cubing Helper

Letter pairs

One word for every pair of memo letters: picture the word instead of saying two letters. X is said “sh”, J is said “y”, and Q is said “qu” or “ch”. Each word starts with the first letter's sound and has the second letter's sound after it.

Our own list, rewritten for this site on 2026-11-11 to your rules: 552 pairs (24 letters × 23). Vivid things, actions and adjectives, crude words allowed, no people or characters. Well-known initials stay as they are (GQ, HQ, IQ, DJ, QR code, BBQ, TV, UFO). No two words mean nearly the same thing (one cat, one frog, one word per rude body part), and no two share a stem. C words never start with “ch” and S words never start with “sh”, because those sounds are Q and X. Swap any word for one that works better for you. Checked by log/2026-10-23-letter-pairs-check.mjs.
