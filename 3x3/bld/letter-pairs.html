Letter pairs

One word or two-word picture for every pair of memo letters: picture it instead of saying two letters. X is said “sh”, J is said “y”, Q is said “qu”.

Our own list, rewritten on 2026-11-19 to your sound rules: 552 pairs (24 letters × 23). Vivid things; suggestive is fine, nothing outright NSFW; no people or characters. Well-known initials keep their letters (PJ, UFO, TV, OK …). Checked by log/2026-11-19-letter-pairs-check.mjs, which tests every rule below on the spelling. Swap any word for one that works better for you.

How a pair is read
KindRuleExamples
One wordStarts with the 1st letter; the 2nd letter is the first and only clear consonant after itBK beak, XD shed, NP nappy
Two wordsThe 1st word starts with the 1st letter, the 2nd word with the 2nd letter. Always for C, Q and vowels as the 2nd letterXC shark cage, QE quirky eagle, BU beach umbrella
KAlways one word with a k sound (k or ck)XK shack, DK duck
Vowel, one wordOnly when there is no other consonantBE bee, SE see
InitialsWell-known letters stay as they arePJ, UFO, TV, AK-47

Sounds that could be read two ways (ph, th, ng, x, a soft c or g) are not used where they decide a letter.
