Orozco step 2

The next step after Orozco: pairs with P in one commutator, the way Orozco already does pairs with B. A short, fixed list; every other pair stays plain Orozco.

Worked out here (2026-11-08), not from your notes or a published list. Every commutator on this page was checked in the cube simulator: it cycles the buffer and the two targets and moves nothing else, and each setup keeps the buffer and the other target where they are. Built by log/2026-11-08-orozco-step2.mjs.

The idea

Orozco is easy because B never moves: D and L turns never touch it, so the setup only ever moves your one letter into O, H or L. L turns never touch P (or the buffer) either, so P can play the same part, with three bases of its own.

Step 2a: three bases, like O H L

The P family: [R U R' , D] with D for L, D' for T and D2 for H. An L turn brings the other letter into L or H.

Step 2b: two more bases

[R' D' R , U2] (with A) and [R' D' R , U'] (with D). P with A, I, S or U (an L turn into A), and A or D with L, T or H (a D turn into P, the same L T H letters with D, D', D2).
