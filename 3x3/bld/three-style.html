How it works

Like Orozco: you memo the corners in pairs from the buffer C, and each pair has one commutator. Every commutator here is a short core with a setup around it (setup in orange):

  1. Setup: moves one or two of the pieces (the buffer and the two targets) into the three spots the core works on. Open any pair to see which pieces go where, with a picture before and after.
  2. Core [A , B] (A, B, A undone, B undone): cycles those three spots, buffer sticker to the 1st spot, 1st to 2nd, 2nd back to the buffer spot.
  3. Undo the setup. Everything else goes back; the two targets are solved.

So learn the cores first (each group below), then the pairs with no setup, then one-move setups, and so on. Same letters as your other blind pages; J and M are the buffer's other stickers.
