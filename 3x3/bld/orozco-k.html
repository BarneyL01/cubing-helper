Cubing Helper

Orozco with helper K

Your Orozco with K in B's place, done from one rotation: y', then Orozco's own insertion [R D R' , U / U' / U2], then y when the corners are done. Rotate once for all the corners; the setups are never more than two moves.

Worked out here (2026-11-17), not from your notes or a published list. K is the other sticker of helper P's corner, so the setups are the same kind as helper P's: L and B turns, which never move the buffer or K. Every commutator was checked in the cube simulator, the y' view was checked to be the same moves, and Orozco with helper K solved 3,000 whole random solves (M2 edges, then these corners). How a solve goes

The idea

  • Memo from C as always. The 1st letter of each pair forwards (buffer → letter → K), the 2nd backwards (buffer → K → letter).
  • Rotate once (y'), do every letter while you hold the cube that way, rotate back (y). Seen after y', the setups are F and L turns (they were L and B before the rotation).
  • After y' the base is [R D R' , U^k]: Orozco's O base insertion, with the U turn picking B, D or A. Without the rotation it is [F D F' , U^k].
