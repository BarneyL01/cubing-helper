1. The corners that are left

Five corners are unsolved. Each target letter below is a sticker on one of the four top-layer corners; the buffer corner DFR holds V, K and P. A pair is two target letters on different corners.

A top-layer corner that is in the right place but twisted is not a pair: use the Twists & parity tab. That tab also has the odd-count step, for when the number of letters in your memo is odd.

2. The setups, simplest first

Every pair is one of the three bases plus a setup of any faces. Setups are shown in orange. The table counts the pairs by setup length and compares with the R, U, F, D only tab.

Learning the setups below first covers the most pairs. Each row lists every pair that uses that setup, with its full commutator: the setup, then the base, then the setup undone.

3. Easiest: put the corners in place, twist later

If you don't mind corners ending up twisted, you only need one commutator per pair of corners, not per pair of stickers: 12 instead of 108. Then fix the twists at the end with your corner-twist algorithm.

  1. Memo by corners, not stickers. Look at the piece in the buffer (DFR): which top corner does it belong in? Then the piece sitting in that corner: where does it belong? Keep going. If the buffer's own piece comes back to the buffer while top corners are still out of place, start again from the first one that is out of place.
  2. Pair them up and do the card below for each pair: it sends the buffer's piece to the first corner and that corner's piece to the second. Which way round a piece ends up doesn't matter yet.
  3. Twist. Every corner is now in its place, some of them twisted. Fix them with your twist algorithm (R' D' R D)×2 U (D' R' D R)×2 U' (U2 or U' for the other top corners, and with R before and R' after to include the buffer corner), or see the Twists & parity tab.

4. Every pair

Each row sends the buffer's piece to the first letter, the piece at the first letter to the second, and the piece at the second back to the buffer. Order matters: AB and BA are different rows.
