Cubing Helper

Block practice

Plan a block in inspection, build it, then check it against the shortest solutions.

Not from your notes. The solutions come from the site's own search: the shortest possible, with U D L R F B M r for the first block and U R M r for the second, so a person usually finds a longer one. Solutions one move longer are shown too (often easier to find). They are generated, so they have no mnemonic words. Block pieces follow the usual Roux first and second blocks. Checked by log/2026-11-19-roux-block-practice-check.mjs (every solution solves its block, lengths equal a full breadth-first search, notes checked move by move). Nothing is saved about how you did: only the current scramble and settings.
