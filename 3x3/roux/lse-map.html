An arrow is any of M' U M, M U M', M' U' M or M U' M' (with a U turn first if needed) that orients every edge. Orientation is judged as on the Reference tab.

How to read a card

  1. Start: the pattern with the centres lined up. Turn U until your cube matches the picture.
  2. Fix (some cards only): a few moves first, or U' M' loops and never reaches an arrow.
  3. U' M' 1, 2, …: the position after each pair. The centres are a quarter turn out after odd pairs; that is expected.
  4. Finish: the arrow is solved and every edge is oriented. Go on to 4b.
