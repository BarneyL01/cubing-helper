3x3 — Repeat Method

A corners-first beginner method where every step is a short group of moves done a few times in a row, like Sassy. Four repeats, no L moves, no M moves.

Designed here (2026-10-29), not from a published method or your notes. A computer search found short move groups whose repeats move only a few pieces; the steps were then checked on 1,000 whole solves in the cube simulator, following the rules on this page. Solving corners first and then edges is the same order as other corners-first methods (Waterman, Roux); the repeats are what is new here. Mnemonic words are given where your letter code has the letters (it has none for D).
