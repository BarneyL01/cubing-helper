Cubing Helper

Overview

The ideas behind algorithms and methods, not the algorithms themselves. Each page takes one idea and shows it on the cube.

Our own summary (2026-10-28), written to decide what to explore next. Pages marked "Page" are built; the rest are listed so we can pick one to look at further. Everything on the built pages is worked out by the simulator and checked by log/2026-10-28-theory-check.mjs.
