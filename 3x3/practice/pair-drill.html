How it works

  1. Level and letter: level 0 means B, D, F or K is in the pair. Level 1: one turn takes a letter into a helper's place (E, O, X to B; G to K; R, L to D; H to F; S to K). Level 2: the letter first turns into S, X, H or L, then goes on as level 1.
  2. Turns: for helper F and K the turns are as seen after the rotation (z for F, y' for K).
  3. Where the other letter is: it moves only if it is on a turned face.
  4. Setup: the helper's usual setup for the letter where the other one is now.
  5. Commutator: rotation, setup, base, undo, rotate back. The exceptions show a shorter one.

Pairs are picked uniformly from the filtered list (every pair equally likely), never the same one twice in a row. The current pair, steps shown, filters and mode are saved. ?pair=QT opens that pair, ?mode=decide opens Decide (the score there is for this visit only).
