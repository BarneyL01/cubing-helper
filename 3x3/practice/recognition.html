Cubing Helper

Recognition practice

A 3D cube shows a last-layer case. Recognise it and do its algorithm on your own cube before the time runs out; then (if you like) the solution shows for a few seconds and the next case comes up.

The cases and algorithms are the ones on the 2-look OLL, 2-look PLL and Roux CMLL pages (your notes). Each case is shown with a random turn of the top layer, as you would meet it in a solve; the solution starts with the top-layer turn to make first. Nothing is recorded: there is no way to check your cube, so this is for recognising cases and getting the moves into your hands. Settings are kept in this browser.
