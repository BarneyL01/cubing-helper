Cubing Helper

Blind drills

Short drills for one skill at a time: the letter scheme, tracing, holding the memo, and small solves you can check. Answers stay hidden until you tap.

Letters are Speffz, memo as on the M2 / Old Pochmann page (edges from DF, corners from UBL). The drill scrambles are made from the Corner cycles and Edge cycles tables: commutators, twists and flips combined until exactly the chosen pieces are unsolved, then reversed. Every scramble is checked on the simulated cube; the whole generator is checked by log/2026-10-23-bld-drill-check.mjs (1,900 scrambles, each solved by the M2 / Old Pochmann moves). Nothing is scored or recorded. Settings and the current drill are kept in this browser.
