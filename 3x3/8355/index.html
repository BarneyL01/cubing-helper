3x3 — 8355 Method

A beginner method that gets by on one algorithm: Sassy (R U R' U'). Layer by layer, but the middle edges go in through a "keyhole" before the last layer is touched.

Not from your own notes: rewritten from the Speedsolving.com Wiki page (see also the forum thread), using your name for R U R' U'. Every algorithm, both worked examples and the last-corners steps were checked with the cube simulator. Where the wiki needed an addition, the step says so.
